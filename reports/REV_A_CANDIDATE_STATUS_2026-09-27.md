## Functional design and open verification

- Four photodiodes are reverse biased relative to the 1.25 V TIA reference; ON and OFF comparator paths feed eight one-shots and the 10-pin FPGA event header.
- Separate `LED_EN`/GND header drives the AO3400A emitter switch from a 3.3 V FPGA pulse train. The 10-pin event header does not carry `LED_EN`; the FPGA must use the separate connection.
- Eight 2 MΩ optional hysteresis resistors are marked DNP.
- These are schematic and datasheet checks, **not measured behavior**. The Rev A gain, threshold margins, false-trigger rate, event width, optical crosstalk, and LED current have not been verified on a populated board.

KiCad Companion Rule 12 requires a circuit simulation with real manufacturer models before finalizing analog/sensor/power hardware. The official OPA381 and REF3312 models are in `simulation/models/`. TI's [TLV3202-Q1 PSpice archive](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV3202-Q1) was downloaded as `simulation/models/sboj021.zip`; its extracted files contain helper subcircuits but no verified ready top-level dual-comparator subcircuit. TI supplies an [HSPICE model for SN74LVC1G123](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC1G123) and [states it will not run directly in PSpice](https://e2e.ti.com/support/logic-group/logic/f/logic-forum/1349442/sn74lvc1g123-one-shot-model-in-pspice). No confirmed usable manufacturer SPICE models were found for VBPW34S, TSAL6200, or AO3400A. Therefore the required board-faithful transient simulation has **not** passed.

## Assembly and order blockers

1. Establish usable manufacturer models or a documented resolution for the simulation gate, then run and record board-faithful DC and transient results.
2. Select order details and assembly scope. The candidate spec has no LCSC assignments; 91 populated schematic entries lack an MPN, including most passives and all connectors/testpoints/mounting items. Mechanical/testpoint items are not all purchased BOM lines, but populated passives and connectors still need exact part selections.
3. Export a fresh manufacturing package only after the release gates. Review Gerber/drill layers, BOM population and DNP handling, and each CPL rotation/polarity in the fabricator's live order preview, especially D1–D4 and D5.
4. Bench verify a first article for optical response, reference/threshold voltages, LED pulse current, one-shot pulse width, and FPGA logic levels before committing to volume.
